## Cross-machine findings

1. **The closure pattern is universal so far.** Four independent
   machines (rank-1, the candidate, and the dip-aware pair in
   progress) close with the same anatomy: two parametric boundary
   families, a derived floor law, a derived kind law. This regularity
   is now a conjecture about the class, not a coincidence.
2. **The branching-constant series: {phi, 2-adic, 1}.** Dossier-3's
   death tree branches at the golden ratio (no-TT constraint); the
   rank-1 tree branches 2-adically in train lengths; Antihydra's cone
   is linear with branching constant exactly 1 (merge branches
   throttled by the a >= 2 guard). Three machines, three constants,
   each derived from its episode-alternation rules.
3. **The golden ratio changes seats.** Dossier-3 carries phi in its
   cone; Antihydra carries 1/phi as its gambler's-ruin hazard root
   (r^3 - 2r + 1 at steps -1/+2). Same constant, different organs.
4. **"Array-class" is often an alphabet artifact.** The two
   architecture variants collapse to bounded counter tuples in the
   (01)-run alphabet; dossiers 7 and 8 carry unary-coded ladders
   (unbounded RLE, fixed counter vector). The class boundary is
   drawn by the machine's natural word alphabet, not by symbol-level
   shape.
5. **The verifier is universal.** The halting game now runs on four
   state-space classes (renewal pairs, five-integer certified states,
   growing words, hydra triples) with one protocol; Antihydra's
   lethal family even has a closed-form halt time,
   (3c^2 + 44c + 125)/4 for odd c.
