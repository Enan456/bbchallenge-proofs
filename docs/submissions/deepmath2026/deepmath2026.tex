\begin{abstract}
Whether the sixth Busy Beaver value can be determined turns on a
residual set of ``cryptid'' Turing machines whose halting problems have
resisted every decider brought to bear on them. We reconceptualize the
halting question for this class as an event-history problem: the
machine's evolution is a long-duration process punctuated by recurrent
events, and halting is a terminal event whose hazard concentrates in
identifiable regions of a low-dimensional state space. Exploiting this
structure, we develop a certified pipeline in which raw tape dynamics
are aggregated into orbit events, embedded in non-Euclidean
(logarithmic, simplex, hyperbolic) coordinate systems where the
dynamics become affine, and then re-derived as machine-checked theorems
in core Lean~4 under a minimal axiom set. Our central result reduces
two previously open BB(6) machines, end to end, to conditional theorems
of the form $\Safe \rightarrow \lnot\Halts$, in which the antecedent
concerns only a deterministic affine map on five integers. We validate
each layer by differential testing against $10^{9}$ raw machine steps
and show that the excluded parameter configurations are lethal:
entering them provably produces the halting transition. The remaining
conjecture is therefore not an artifact of the method but the halting
problem itself, compressed to arithmetic. We close by situating the
approach within the formal-mathematics workflow emerging around Lean
and large language models.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and framing}
The determination of $BB(5)=47{,}176{,}870$, machine-checked in Rocq by
the bbchallenge Collaboration \cite{coqbb5,deciders}, transformed the
Busy Beaver program from a computational pastime into a subfield of
verified mathematics. The frontier now rests at $n=6$, where the
obstruction is qualitative rather than quantitative: a small residual
set of machines---the cryptids, of which Antihydra is the
best-known---encode Collatz-like arithmetic, so that deciding them
appears to require number theory beyond current reach \cite{aaronson}.
Tao's result that almost all Collatz orbits attain almost bounded
values \cite{tao-collatz} marks the present boundary of what is
provable for such maps, and it is a measure-theoretic statement; the
Busy Beaver question demands pointwise control of a single orbit.

Our point of departure is methodological. Treating the machine as a
dynamical system in the tradition of Moore and K\r{u}rka
\cite{moore,kurka}, we observe that for the compact-growth cryptids the
hazard of halting is not diffuse in time: it concentrates at
\emph{incision events}, rare configurations in which a distinguished
probe state reads the one symbol that could initiate the halting
transition. The natural framework is that of event-history analysis:
long censored spells of routine motion, recurrent events with
measurable covariates, occasional regime transitions (renewals), and a
terminal event whose risk set we can characterize exactly. The
methodological task is then to certify (i) that the risk set is where
we say it is, and (ii) what the process does between visits to it.

We therefore aggregate. Record-level analysis of the tape yields, for
each machine, a shape vector: the field boundaries and the positions of
two mobile ``holes,'' summarized as counters $(a,b,c)$. Embedding the
resulting orbit in three non-Euclidean charts---the componentwise
logarithmic map of Maslov--Litvinov dequantization \cite{viro,litvinov},
the projective simplex normalization of the Perron--Frobenius
tradition, and a hyperbolic shape-versus-scale chart in the spirit of
Poincar\'e embeddings \cite{nickel-kiela}---renders the dynamics
affine (Figure~\ref{fig:charts}). Generic evolution appears as line
segments; renewals appear as corners and jumps. It is this piecewise
affinity, discovered geometrically, that the certification layer then
proves.

\section{Certification: from charts back to theorems}
The geometric layer guides but does not warrant. Every claim used in
the final theorems is re-derived in core Lean~4, with axioms restricted
to $\{\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}\}$, over the raw
transition-table semantics validated against the Rocq lineage by parser
identities, differential execution traces, and a mechanized
bisimulation with a zipper-tape model.

The certified account of the two machines' dynamics is an
\emph{interaction calculus}: a ten-step caterpillar gait crawls the
field; static holes are crossed ($+4$) or terminated ($+6$) according
to the parity of arrival; adjacent holes fuse ($+9$); the field edge
executes a parity-dependent extension. Seven episode theorems per
machine---each a complete trajectory between successive spawn entries,
with exact step counts and probe safety---are compiled from this
calculus by simulation-driven generators and glued by two inductions
(an $n$-fold cycle lemma and a mixed sweep lemma whose parity
constraint explains every shift constant). A five-integer abstract
orbit $\mathsf{metaStep}$ dispatches the episodes; a transport theorem,
a segment-induction keystone, and fully concrete startup certificates
(235 and 2{,}044 steps, including one degenerate episode per machine)
complete the chain.

\begin{theorem}
For each of the two machines, at axioms
$\{\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}\}$:
$\ \Safe \rightarrow \lnot\,\Halts(\mathsf{blankStart})$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Validation and the status of the conjecture}
Three validation regimes discipline the pipeline. First, the empirical
calculus is fail-closed: it must classify every episode exactly, and it
does so for $10^{9}$ raw steps per machine with no unexplained events.
Second, the Lean $\mathsf{metaStep}$ is mirrored in Python and checked
snapshot-for-snapshot against raw emulation, a test now permanent in
the repository. Third, the abstract orbits are run to $10^{8}$
episodes (a raw-step horizon near $10^{15}$) without leaving the
guarded region.

The substantive status of $\Safe$ deserves emphasis
(Figure~\ref{fig:gamma}). The renewal map is a subtractive
Euclid--Collatz hybrid: generic loops conserve $\gamma=c-b$ while $a$
advances; deep renewals exchange the roles of $a$ and $b$; fusions
rotate $(a,2,c)\mapsto(a{+}9,c{-}6,1)$. We verified by direct
construction that the excluded configurations halt within roughly
fifty steps. The conjecture is therefore sharp: no weaker invariant
suffices, and no stronger one is available without resolving the
arithmetic. What has changed is the type of the open problem---from a
question about a Turing machine to a question about a five-integer
recurrence.

\section{Relation to the emerging formal workflow}
The development is an instance of the human--AI--kernel workflow that
Tao and collaborators have articulated for the Equational Theories
Project and the Palomar registry \cite{etp,palomar}: generators (here,
an AI assistant compiling proofs from validated empirical calculi) are
labor; the Lean kernel is the sole trust surface. Our experience adds
two proof-engineering observations of possible independent use:
\texttt{omega} imports classical choice through negated-conjunction
hypotheses, and rewriting on \texttt{stepN} goals triggers
whole-run definitional evaluation---both eliminable by constructive
De Morgan lemmas and term-mode composition, respectively.

\paragraph{Honest scope.} No unconditional non-halting claim is made
for any open machine. The charts are instruments of discovery and
exposition; no theorem depends on them. Empirical horizons are bounded
by computation performed, not by what is true.
