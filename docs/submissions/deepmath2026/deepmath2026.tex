\begin{abstract}
The fifth Busy Beaver number was settled in 2024; the sixth is guarded
by a residual set of ``cryptid'' Turing machines whose halting problems
resist every known decider. We present a certified pipeline that hunts
these cryptids geometrically: raw tape dynamics are compressed into
\emph{orbit events}, embedded in non-Euclidean (logarithmic, simplex,
hyperbolic) charts where their self-similar structure becomes affine,
and then re-certified in core Lean~4 (axioms
$\{\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}\}$ only). The headline result:
two previously open BB(6) machines are reduced, machine-checked end to
end, to conditional theorems $\Safe \rightarrow \lnot\Halts$, where
$\Safe$ concerns a deterministic affine map on five integers. Every raw
step either machine ever takes---startup, one degenerate episode, and an
eternity of seven certified episode types---is accounted for by
theorems; the remaining conjecture is a subtractive Euclid/Collatz
hybrid whose excluded parameter bands are \emph{provably lethal}, so
avoiding them is exactly the halting question. The proofs were compiled
by generator--verifier loops: fail-closed empirical calculi validated
against $10^9$ raw steps, then emitted as Lean and re-checked by the
kernel.
\end{abstract}

\section{Cryptids and the shape of the residual set}
After BB(5) $=47{,}176{,}870$ was machine-checked, attention moved to
the BB(6) frontier, where a residual cohort of holdout machines---%
``cryptids'' such as Antihydra---encode Collatz-like arithmetic. We
classified 1{,}094 holdouts with a Collatz-lens profiler (growth
dimension, record structure, exact projections); none is decided by
existing budgets, and 51 exhibit \emph{compact growth}: tape
configurations that stay describable by a bounded vector of counters.
These are the machines our pipeline targets.

\section{Incision geometry: checking only at the boundary}
For a compact-growth machine, halting danger concentrates at
\emph{incision events}: the rare configurations where a distinguished
``probe'' state reads the symbol that could launch the halting
transition. In Lean we prove this is not a heuristic but an
equivalence: for Antihydra, an event process $\mathsf{eorbit}$ (a
closed-form \emph{volley} skipping through the interior dynamics)
satisfies
$$\mathsf{SafeOrbit} \iff \forall k,\ \mathsf{eorbit}(k)\neq\bot,$$
so checking safety \emph{only at incisions} is sound and complete.
Embedding event clouds via $x\mapsto\log(1{+}x)$, projective simplex
normalization, and upper-half-plane charts renders the record dynamics
affine and exposes family structure across the cohort---the empirical
atlas that guides which machines admit exact certification.

\section{Orbital dragons: predictive emulation}
An \emph{orbit finder} fits fail-closed exact rational affine
specifications to record-level dynamics (exact arithmetic, no
tolerance; any residual rejects the spec). On the nine-machine
certification queue it returns exact specs for four machines:
Antihydra, one known reduction target, and two machines new to this
program (``dossier-2/3,'' a mirror pair). A generic predictor then
replays orbits at record granularity, giving certified skip horizons
and, crucially, the complete grammar of each machine's motion.

\section{The interaction calculus and conditional closure}
For the mirror pair we decode the entire eternal dynamics into a
\emph{local interaction calculus} on a tape shape
$1^{a}\,0\,1^{b}\,0\,1^{c}$: a ten-step ``caterpillar'' gait crawls the
field; static holes are \emph{crossed} ($+4$) or \emph{terminated}
($+6$) according to arrival parity; adjacent holes \emph{fuse} ($+9$);
the field edge runs a parity-dependent business. Validated
episode-for-episode against $10^{9}$ raw steps per machine, the
calculus compiles to seven Lean episode theorems per machine over a
uniform \texttt{holeTape} shape, glued by an $n$-fold cycle induction
and a mixed sweep lemma whose $F$-parity constraint explains every
$+4/{+}6/{+}9$ shift. A five-integer abstract orbit
$\mathsf{metaStep}$ dispatches the episodes; transport, a segment
keystone, and fully concrete startup anchors (235 and 2{,}044 certified
steps, including one degenerate episode each) yield, at axioms
$\{\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}\}$:
\begin{theorem}
For each of the two machines,\quad
$\Safe \;\longrightarrow\; \lnot\,\Halts(\mathsf{blankStart})$.
\end{theorem}
$\Safe$ states that the abstract orbit never leaves its guarded region.
The renewal map is a subtractive Euclid/Collatz hybrid: generic loops
conserve $\gamma = c-b$ while $a$ grows by $6$; deep renewals swap
$(a,b)$; fusions rotate $(a,2,c)\mapsto(a{+}9,\,c{-}6,\,1)$.
Simulating the excluded bands ($\gamma\in\{1,3,5\}$ at renewal,
$b\le 2$ at carried entries) produces a halt within ${\sim}50$ steps:
the conjecture \emph{is} the halting problem, compressed from a Turing
machine to arithmetic on five integers. Both orbits survive $10^{8}$
abstract episodes ($\approx 10^{15}$ raw steps).

\section{Method: generator--verifier proof compilation}
All certified layers are emitted by simulation-driven compilers: atoms
are derived by running the machine table over declared precondition
windows (the theorem cannot diverge from the machine); episodes are
compiled from segment plans with funext normalizations between stages;
mirror machines are generated by systematic sign-flips of the plans.
The empirical layer is fail-closed---any unexplained episode halts the
pipeline---and everything emitted is re-checked by the Lean kernel, so
the generators are convenience, not trust surface. Notable
proof-engineering findings: \texttt{omega} leaks
$\mathrm{Classical.choice}$ from negated-conjunction hypotheses;
and rewriting on \texttt{stepN} goals triggers a post-rewrite
definitional-equality attempt that evaluates entire runs (a hidden
quadratic cost), eliminated by term-mode run gluing.

\section{Honest scope}
No unconditional non-halting claim is made for any open machine. Four
machines (Antihydra, its companion, and the mirror pair) are reduced to
single conjectures; for the mirror pair the conjecture is a
deterministic five-integer recurrence, sharper in kind than its
Collatz-like relatives. Empirical horizons are bounded by computation,
not by what is true.
