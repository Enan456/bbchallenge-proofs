\section*{The eighteen closures}

Each machine below carries a theorem checked by the Lean 4 kernel at
axioms $\{\mathrm{propext}, \mathrm{Quot.sound}\}$, with no
\texttt{sorry}: \emph{if its explicit arithmetic conjecture holds,
the machine never halts from the blank tape}. Each reduction is
complete (startup, every episode, and the transport to the abstract
orbit are theorems) and, where tested, sharp (states violating the
conjecture provably halt within tens of raw steps).

\section*{How each was closed}

The method is uniform. \textbf{Watch}: sample the machine at a
recurring configuration; the tape at those events has a bounded
description (five integers for dossier-2/3, a hydra triple, one hole
and two counters for Dvisetu, a word of blocks for rank-1).
\textbf{Compress}: derive the exact law of that description
empirically and fail-closed; every transition and its exact step
count must be reproduced, any miss aborts. Evidence grades at
freezing: dossier-2/3 validated to $10^{9}$ raw steps; Dvisetu
2{,}746/2{,}746 timed transitions at $2\times10^{7}$; rank-1
2{,}179/2{,}186 frozen at $2\times10^{8}$ with the residue
characterized, plus exact reseed differentials. \textbf{Certify}:
compile the law into Lean: raw semantics, a halt-localization
corridor lemma (each machine funnels every halt through one probe
pattern), gait-level episode theorems with exact step counts,
inductions for the unbounded middles, a concrete startup anchor, and
a transport theorem showing the abstract orbit predicts the machine
forever. The conjecture is then a statement about an integer (or
word) sequence, and the theorem converts it into non-halting.

Two engineering results from the newest closures: Dvisetu's one
genuinely hard branch (the recursive divide renewal) is folded into
its conjecture as a named, documented assumption rather than proven;
rank-1, by contrast, needed \emph{no} such fold: at cell-level grain
its boundary families and decision function are subsumed and proven,
making it the first closure on a state space of growing dimension.
Dossiers 7 through 18 landed by the same pipeline taken to its
logical end: their certified engines run at single-raw-step grain, so every
branch of their record laws (including every residue case unobserved
at the empirical horizon) is simulated exactly and proven faithful;
no assumption is folded anywhere, and the only fail-closed exits are
the two genuine halt patterns and fuel. Design mirrors validated at $2\times10^{7}$ raw steps per machine
(from 3{,}890 to 10{,}909 record transitions each, all exact) before
any Lean was written; the final tranche closed the dip-aware pair,
both architecture variants (one mirror spanning a 13M-step regime
shift), and the two-sided five including the twin pair, whose law is
certified twice from one file. All eighteen keystones replay in a
single build at the two axioms.

\section*{What it takes to push, validate, and get approved}

\paragraph{1. External formal validation (Palomar).} Prepared and
pinned: nineteen entries (the original five plus one per new
closure) with challenge/solution comparator pairs whose challenge
files import Lean core only. Local intake: 0 failures, 0 warnings.
\emph{Remaining action: one browser session by the author}
(GitHub authentication; the registry re-checks the proofs at the
pinned commit and publishes the exact statements). Effort: under an
hour. This satisfies the formal-proof-validation requirement of the
community process.

\paragraph{2. Community publication and reproduction
(bbchallenge.org).} The community's adopted process: publish on the
forum, invite reproduction, formal validation by a non-author, with
an announcement-and-objection convention. A forum post is drafted
(\texttt{docs/submissions/bbchallenge-forum-post.md}): reduction
framing, explicit non-claims (zero machines decided), and a
one-command reproduction
(\texttt{git checkout <pinned SHA> \&\& lake build}). \emph{Remaining
actions: the author posts it; one independent person runs the build}
(minutes of their time; the kernel does the validation). The
scale criterion ($\ge$10\% of undecided machines) applies to bulk
deciders, not cryptid results; a sound invariant search over every
learned law in the cohort returned zero invariant-decidable
machines, so conditional reduction is the demonstrated frontier for
this class.

\paragraph{3. Conference review (DeepMath 2026).} The extended
abstract (v18) is complete with the census, the six closures, the
verification-game results, and honest scope; form fields are
prepared. \emph{Remaining actions: the author's OpenReview profile
and submission click}; then peer review runs its course.

\paragraph{4. What approval cannot mean.} No process turns these
into unconditional results: each conjecture is a Collatz-class orbit
avoidance statement, and the program's own negative results
(no congruence invariant to modulus 384; ranking-class certificates
exhaustively refuted on the true fatal model) show why. The honest
end state of approval is: eighteen machine-checked reductions, externally
re-verified, publicly reproduced, with each machine's halting
problem restated as one explicit arithmetic question.
