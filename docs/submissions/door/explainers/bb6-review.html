The BB(6) renewal map:
an almost-all non-halting theorem and its harmonic-analytic core

A holdout of the Σ(6) Busy-Beaver search reduces to an explicit Gauss-type Markov map. The measure form of the almost-all theorem is proved; the natural-density form is reduced to one open lemma — a modulus-uniform spectral gap for a twisted transfer operator. This page states the reduction precisely, labels every step by proof status, and lets you recompute the load-bearing objects from the exact machine.

proved proved · feeds the conditional result open — the single wall

1The map, the section, the theorem

States are pairs (a,b) ∈ ℤ≥12. Put β(a)=a−1 if a odd, a−2 if a even (always even); (α,β)=(6,a−1) or (9,a−2). The renewal map:

R(a,b) =  (a+6, b−3) [b even, T] ;  (α+3(b−3), β−(b−3)) [b odd, b<β, C] ;  (α+3β+3, b−β−4) [b odd, b>β, F]

The fatal set is 𝒟 = {b≤2} ∪ {b=4} ∪ {b odd, b>β, o:=b−β ∈ {1,3,5}}; the machine halts iff the orbit meets 𝒟. Deciding a single seed is Collatz-hard; we treat the generic seed.

On the section S={b>β, b odd} the first-return map G is, in the coordinate z=1−a/b, a countable full-branch piecewise-Möbius Markov map of [0,1]. Its branch endpoints obey an exact recurrence with an algebraic accumulation point:

ρk+1 = (3ρk+1)−1,   ρ0=1  →  ρ* = (√13−1)/6,   the root of 3ρ2+ρ−1=0.
Theorem 1a — Measure formproved
Lebesgue-almost every real seed is non-halting; equivalently the Lebesgue measure of seeds halting from scale ≥ a★ tends to 0. Every integer orbit has a+b → ∞.
Theorem 1b — Density formconditional
The natural density of halting integer seeds is 0 — conditional on the uniform-gap lemma (§4). Directly confirmed numerically: the halting fraction falls like C/N.

2The reduction, by proof status

Click any node for the precise statement and proof idea. The green chain is unconditional; the amber chain feeds Theorem 1b; the red node is the sole wall.

proved  Parity grammar ⇒ no intermittency

β(a) is even, so T outputs odd, C outputs even, F outputs odd: the grammar is T→{C,F}, C→T, F→{C,F}; TT never occurs. Exhaustive parity identity. The parabolic generator is never iterated — no Pomeau–Manneville intermittency.

proved  Branch partition & full-branch Markov

Endpoints ρk=|sk/rk| for Pk=CℓkFℓ, by induction via c−1(y)=(3y+1)−1; accumulation ρ*. Each branch maps onto (0,∞) (Markov). formal write-out pending the tiling is verified numerically over 1.5×106 returns.

proved  Eventual expansion inf|(G²)′| = 3

|G′(z)| = 3k+1/(rk(1−z)+sk)2. inf|G′|=1 attained only as z→0+ on branch k=1 (the fatal-cusp approach); z=0 maps to z=1 ∈ branch k=0 where |G′|≥3. No neutral cycle ⇒ inf|(G²)′|=3.

proved  Summable variation & ACIM + mixing

var(1/|G′|) ≈ 1.64 < ∞, geometric tail (ratio 3ρ*²≈0.566). Rychlik ⇒ unique ACIM μ ∼ Leb, density h ∈ BV, exponential decay of correlations. cited Rychlik 1983.

proved  Transience — a+b ↗ ∞

Elementary and unconditional: a+b strictly increases every step (T:+3; C:+(b∓1); F:+(a+3/+4)), all ≥2. Holds for every orbit, incl. the certified seed.

proved  Hazard C=6h(0), summable

Fatal ⟺ z ∈ [0, 6/S] at scale S; by mixing the crossing law has density h, so per-crossing hazard = 6h(0)/S(1+o(1)), 0<C≤6‖h‖∞<∞. Borel–Cantelli over the geometrically growing scales ⇒ Theorem 1a.

amber  Model → exact map (truncation)

Exact generators = linear parts + fixed translations (sup-norm ≤9). Bulk (k≤M(a)=O(log a)) close to O(1/a); tail measure O(1/a), strong expansion, absorbed into a uniform Lasota–Yorke inequality; spectral-gap persistence by cited Keller–Liverani. write-out tail-operator constant.

open  Density transfer ⇒ requires the uniform gap

The halting set H is dense with positive-measure complement, hence not Jordan-measurable — naive equidistribution (Weyl / Koksma–Hlawka) fails. The density form reduces to equidistribution of integer orbits inside fixed cylinders, i.e. to the uniform-gap lemma of §4.

4The open lemma — a modulus-uniform gap

Lemma (open) — the harmonic-analytic coreopen
supM maxt≠0,M/2 rM(t) < 1: the twisted operators have a spectral gap bounded away from 0 uniformly in the modulus M and non-trivial character t.

Status of its ingredients. Twisted Lasota–Yorke proved (L¹-contraction exact; variation constant degrades like 1+|t| — the crux). Aperiodicity proved two ways, incl. an exact cycle-cohomology test with explicit non-coboundary witnesses to M≤384. Fixed-M gap proved via finite-extension theory over the mixing base. Uniform-in-M gap open.

By the twisted LY it is equivalent to defeating the (1+|t|) degradation by oscillatory cancellation — a Dolgopyat estimate via a uniform-non-integrability (UNI) bound at native resolution. Two features put it outside settled theory: the cost is a multiplicative coordinate, not a Birkhoff sum (so Baladi–Vallée does not apply), and the branch matrices have det = ±3, non-unimodular (so homogeneous-dynamics is out). The frequency profile below is flat and resonance-free (sup ≈ 0.082) — evidence the gap is not blocked, not that it is uniform.

7What remains for a complete proof

  • One genuine open problem. The modulus-uniform gap (§4): a Dolgopyat/UNI estimate for a multiplicative-coordinate cost over a non-unimodular Gauss-type map. Neither Baladi–Vallée nor homogeneous dynamics applies off the shelf; likely needs a new idea. This is the whole difficulty.
  • Formal write-outs (no gaps of substance). The full-branch tiling of [0,1]; every constant in the tail-operator LY bound; the assembly uniform-gap ⇒ hf(s)≤Cs−δ ⇒ scale-decomposition ⇒ o(N²); the exact integrals for C=6h(0) and the drift.
  • Standard citations (hypotheses verified). Rychlik; Keller–Liverani; Aaronson–Denker / Parry–Pollicott.
  • Already rigorous. Grammar, endpoints & √13 accumulation, expansion, summable variation, transience, aperiodicity (with witnesses), twisted LY, fixed-M equidistribution.

Bottom line. Theorem 1a is complete at research-paper standard (two standard write-outs aside). Theorem 1b needs exactly one new result — the uniform gap — plus formalization. All of it is the almost-all statement; the specific certified orbit is Collatz-hard and permanently out of scope.
