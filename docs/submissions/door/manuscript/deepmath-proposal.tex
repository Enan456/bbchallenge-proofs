\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Almost-all non-halting for Busy-Beaver renewal maps}\\[2pt]
{\small A transfer-operator attack on the BB(6) cryptid cohort, in the style of Tao's Collatz theorem}\\[2pt]
{\footnotesize BB(6) research program \quad\textbullet\quad 2026-09-12}
\end{center}
\vspace{-2pt}

\noindent\textbf{Problem.} The determination of $\mathrm{BB}(6)$ is obstructed
by a cohort of $\sim\!122$ ``cryptid'' holdout machines---Antihydra the
flagship---whose halting is equivalent to a Collatz-type first-passage event for
an explicit integer map~\cite{Aaronson2020,bbchallenge2024}. Deciding any single
such orbit is Collatz-hard. We pursue the statistical question instead, exactly
as Tao did for Collatz~\cite{Tao2022}: among all $N^2$ integer seeds, what
fraction halt?

\noindent\textbf{Approach.} Each machine's hurler/catcher macro-dynamics reduces
to a piecewise-affine \emph{renewal map} $\R$ on $\mathbb Z_{\ge1}^2$ whose
first return to a section is a countable full-branch \emph{Gauss-type expanding
Markov map} $G$ (branch endpoints $\rho_{k+1}=(3\rho_k+1)^{-1}$, accumulation
$\rho^\ast=(\sqrt{13}-1)/6$). Halting $\iff$ the orbit meets a fatal set $\mathcal D$.
To this object we apply the modern transfer-operator stack: Lasota--Yorke/Rychlik
for the invariant measure~\cite{LasotaYorke1973,Rychlik1983}, a Dolgopyat-type
uniform spectral gap for the twisted operator~\cite{Dolgopyat1998,Naud2005}, and
the Nagaev--Guivarc'h local limit theorem~\cite{HennionHerve2001,AaronsonDenker2001},
with Keller--Liverani stability for the model-to-exact and countable-branch
truncations~\cite{KellerLiverani1999}.

\noindent\textbf{Results.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Measure form (unconditional).} Lebesgue-almost every real seed is
non-halting; every orbit has $a+b\to\infty$. Proved via Rychlik's
theorem~\cite{Rychlik1983}, all hypotheses (full branches, eventual expansion
$\inf|(G^2)'|=3$, summable variation) verified from the exact branch arithmetic.
\item \emph{Density form (reduced).} $\#\{\text{halts}\le N\}=O(N\log N)=o(N^2)$,
conditional on the spectral gap of Naud's Theorem~2.3~\cite{Naud2005}---whose
non-local-integrability hypothesis we \emph{verify in closed form} (the branch
roofs $L_k(\xi)=(r_k-p_k)+(s_k-q_k)\xi$ are pairwise non-proportional,
slopes $-\rho_k$ distinct)---and the Nagaev--Guivarc'h LLT ($\sigma^2\approx0.265>0$,
non-lattice, both verified). The countable-branch gap reduces to the finite-branch
one via an \emph{exact} geometric tail (rate $\lambda^{-1}=1-\rho^\ast=3\rho^{\ast2}$);
the gap holds uniformly in scale because the Dolgopyat hypotheses are
scale-independent algebra of the branch matrices.
\item \emph{Remaining core.} The density form rests, finally, on one arithmetic
exponential-sum estimate---the direct analogue of Tao's
characteristic-function bound on $\mathbb Z/3^n\mathbb Z$~\cite{Tao2022}. A direct
probe shows the relevant cross-cylinder cancellation is \emph{friendly} (empirical
sum at the sampling-noise floor, resonances only at low 3-adic frequencies),
reframing the estimate from adversarial to hard-but-standard.
\item \emph{Mechanization.} A fail-closed \emph{grammar extractor} recovers each
machine's symbolic catch-grammar and its forcing parity invariant with a
Lean-checkable completeness obligation (validated on the renewal map: it
re-derives the proven grammar and the non-intermittency that certifies the
quadratic fuel bound). This scales the $18$ existing kernel-checked conditional
closures (\,$\mathrm{Safe}\to\neg\mathrm{Halt}$, axioms $\{$propext, Quot.sound$\}$\,)
toward the full cohort.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Honest status \& what we seek.} The measure form is complete
and unconditional; the density form is a rigorous reduction to cited theorems
with every dynamical hypothesis discharged, open only at the one arithmetic
estimate above; single-machine halting remains Collatz-hard and untouched. This
is the three-tier posture of Tao's theorem relative to Collatz. We seek feedback
on (i) closing the cross-cylinder exponential-sum estimate, and (ii) the
grammar-certified decider as a route to cohort-scale conditional closure.
