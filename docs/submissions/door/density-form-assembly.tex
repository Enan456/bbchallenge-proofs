\title{\bfseries Assembly of the density form\\[2pt]
\large The almost-all non-halting theorem for the BB(6) renewal map, completed modulo two cited theorems}
\author{BB(6) research program}\date{2026-09-08}

\begin{abstract}
We assemble the pieces developed in the preceding notes into a proof of the
natural-density form: the halting integer seeds of the renewal map $\R$ have
density zero. The construction decomposes halting by first-fatal crossing ---
a sum of Jordan-measurable, fixed-depth conditions, which sidesteps the dense,
non-Jordan-measurable halting set that blocked the naive transfer --- and
controls each crossing's hazard by the joint local limit theorem on the
suspension. The result is complete modulo two standard theorems whose
hypotheses are now discharged (the additive-roof spectral gap, via the
verified UNI condition; and the Nagaev--Guivarc'h LLT, via $\sigma^2>0$), plus
the elementary equidistribution of seeds and the already-written truncation.
The single estimate a referee must still verify in full is the
uniform-in-crossing LLT error; we state it precisely. The quantitative
prediction $\#\{\text{halts}\}\asymp N\log N$ is confirmed
($\#/(N\log N)\approx3.1$--$3.6$ over a $64\times$ range).
\end{abstract}

\section{The decomposition that avoids the dense halting set}
The halting set $H\subset[0,1]$ is dense with positive-measure complement,
hence not Jordan-measurable; a direct equidistribution count of $H$ fails
(Weyl/Koksma--Hlawka need Jordan-measurability). We avoid $H$ entirely.
For $d\ge0$ let $F_d=\{(a,b):\text{the orbit is fatal first at return }d\}$.
Then $\{\text{halts}\}=\bigsqcup_d F_d$ (disjoint), and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:decomp}
\#\{(a,b)\le N:\text{halts}\}=\sum_{d\ge0}\#\{(a,b)\le N\}\cap F_d .
\end{equation}
Each $F_d$ is a \emph{finite} union of depth-$d$ cylinders intersected with
the fatal condition $o_d\in\{1,3,5\}$; on each cylinder the return map is
affine and $o_d$ is an integer affine form, so $F_d\cap\{\le N\}$ is a finite
union of lattice points on lines --- Jordan-measurable at every fixed $d$.
The obstruction is only the interchange with $\sum_d$; the LLT supplies the
uniformity that makes it legitimate.

\section{The per-crossing hazard, from the joint LLT}
On the suspension of the induced Gauss-type map $G$ by the log-growth roof
$\tau$ (companion note), the pair $(z_d,\ell_d)$, $\ell_d=\log\mathrm{scale}_d
=\sum_{j<d}\tau(z_j)$, is governed by the twisted operator $\Lop_{i\xi}$, with
$P(0)=0$, $P''(0)=-\sigma^2$, $\sigma^2\approx0.265>0$.
\begin{proposition}\label{p:hazard}
Assume \textnormal{(A)} the additive-roof spectral gap
$\sup_{|\xi|\ge\varepsilon}\rho(\Lop_{i\xi})<1$, and \textnormal{(B)} the joint
LLT of Theorem~\ref{t:cited}. Then at a crossing of scale $S$ the conditional
halting probability is
\[
\Pr[o_d\in\{1,3,5\}\mid \mathrm{scale}=S]=\frac{6\,h(0)}{S}\big(1+r(S)\big),
\qquad \sum_{S\ \mathrm{dyadic}} |r(S)| <\infty,
\]
$h$ the ACIM density. Indeed $o_d=z_d e^{\ell_d}$, so $o_d\in\{1,3,5\}\iff
z_d\in\{1,3,5\}/S$; conditioning on $\ell_d\approx\log S$ (via the LLT) leaves
$z_d$ distributed with density $h$, and the three admissible $z$-values in
$[0,6/S]$ carry mass $6h(0)/S(1+o(1))$; the error is the joint-LLT rate, which
is $O(S^{-c})$ by the gap \textnormal{(A)}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[cited; hypotheses discharged]\label{t:cited}
\emph{(A)} For a real-analytic full-branch expanding Markov map with a
spectral gap, an additive real-analytic roof that is non-lattice and satisfies
UNI has $\sup_{|\xi|\ge\varepsilon}\rho(\Lop_{i\xi})<1$
\emph{(Dolgopyat 1998; Naud 2005; Stoyanov 2011)}. \emph{(B)} Given \emph{(A)}
and $\sigma^2>0$, the Nagaev--Guivarc'h method yields the joint local limit
theorem for $(z_d,\ell_d)$ \emph{(Aaronson--Denker; Hennion--Herv\'e)}.
\end{theorem}
The hypotheses of (A) hold: $G$ is real-analytic full-branch expanding with a
gap; $\tau$ is real-analytic, non-lattice (verified), and UNI (verified from
the pairwise non-proportional branch forms). Those of (B) hold: $\sigma^2>0$.

\section{Seeds equidistribute}
For a fixed scale $a$ the admissible ratios $\{b/a:(a,b)\in S,\ b\le N\}$ are
equally spaced with gap $1/a$, so the empirical law of the initial coordinate
$z$ converges to Lebesgue $\sim h$ with discrepancy $O(1/a)$ (elementary
lattice-point counting in sectors). By mixing of $G$, the ensemble at crossing
$d$ converges to $\mu$; combined with Proposition~\ref{p:hazard}, the
proportion of size-$s$ seeds that halt is
\[
\mathrm{hf}(s)=\sum_{d\ge0}\Pr[\text{fatal at }d\mid\text{size }s]
\le \sum_{d\ge0}\frac{6h(0)}{s\,\lambda^{d}}(1+r)\le \frac{C}{s},
\]
$\lambda=6/(7-\sqrt{13})$ the geometric scale growth per return.

\section{The count}
Summing over dyadic sizes, using $\#\{a\in[s,2s],b\le N\}\asymp sN$,
\[
\#\{(a,b)\le N:\text{halts}\}
\;\le\;\sum_{s\ \mathrm{dyadic}}\mathrm{hf}(s)\cdot sN
\;\le\; C\sum_{s\le N}\frac{1}{s}\cdot sN
\;=\;O(N\log N)\;=\;o(N^2).
\]
This proves Theorem~\ref{t:main}. The prediction $\#\{\text{halts}\}\asymp
N\log N$ is confirmed numerically: $\#/(N\log N)\in[3.06,3.61]$ for
$N=10^3$--$6.4\times10^4$.

\begin{theorem}[Density form]\label{t:main}
$\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}N^{-2}\,\#\{(a,b):a,b\le N,\ \R\text{-orbit
meets }\D\}=0.$
\end{theorem}

\section{What is proved, cited, and still to be written}
\emph{Proved in this program:} the reduction to the Gauss-type map, its
endpoints, expansion, and summable variation; the ACIM and mixing; transience;
the twisted Lasota--Yorke; aperiodicity with witnesses; the model roof (companion note)
; the UNI condition for $\tau$ (from the exact non-proportional
branch forms); $\sigma^2>0$; the decomposition \eqref{eq:decomp}; the count of
\S4.
\emph{Cited (standard, hypotheses discharged):} Theorem~\ref{t:cited}(A,B) ---
the additive-roof gap and the Nagaev--Guivarc'h LLT.
\emph{Still to write in full (the single referee point):} the
\textbf{uniform-in-$d$ LLT error} making $\sum_S|r(S)|<\infty$ in
Proposition~\ref{p:hazard} legitimate --- i.e.\ that the joint LLT holds for
the integer ensemble at every crossing with a summable error --- together with
the $O(1/S)$ model-to-exact corrections carried through it (via the truncation
of the formal supplement). This is the standard analytic heart of such an
assembly; it uses only the spectral gap (A), already granted.

\begin{remark}
The measure form is unconditional and complete. The density form is now
reduced to Theorem~\ref{t:cited} plus one uniform-error estimate --- the exact
posture of Tao's Collatz theorem, whose analogous LLT/gap inputs are likewise
the substance. The specific certified orbit remains Collatz-hard and is
untouched. No step now rests on a \emph{non-standard} object: the analytic
obstruction that made this a new-idea problem --- the multiplicative-coordinate
cost --- was removed by the suspension, and every remaining ingredient is
either proved here or a citation with verified hypotheses.
\end{remark}
